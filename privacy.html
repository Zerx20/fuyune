Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Fuyune stores only what its features need, keeps it on one server we rent, and never sells it.

📦What we store

WhatWhyHow long
Discord user, server, channel and role IDsSo features work in the right placeWhile the bot is in the server
XP and level for each memberLevels, /rank and /leaderboardUntil the server asks us to delete it
Warnings (reason, who gave it, when)ModerationUntil cleared with /clearwarnings or deleted on request
Server settings (channels, welcome and goodbye text, rules, roles, announcement style, AI personality)So admins can set up the botUntil the server asks us to delete them
Ticket transcripts (the last 100 messages)Saved to the server's own staff-log channel when a ticket closesIn that Discord channel, controlled by the server's staff
Giveaway entriesPicking winners30 days after the giveaway ends
Recent AI chat (the last 10 messages in a channel)So AI replies follow the conversationIn memory only, gone when the bot restarts
Premium purchases (your Discord user ID, the servers it's on in, Stripe checkout ID, amount)To unlock Premium in your servers and handle refundsAs long as the law requires for payment records
Control panel login: with Discord, your user ID, name, avatar and which servers you manage. With Google, your name, email address and profile pictureTo show you only your own serversIn memory only, up to 7 days

What we don't store

Message content (except the AI chat memory and ticket transcripts above), passwords, or card details. Stripe handles payments under Stripe's privacy policy.

This website

The website has no ads or trackers. The control panel uses one login cookie, only after you log in. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when you open a page.

🤝Who we share it with

  • Groq (our AI provider): when someone @mentions the bot or uses /ask, their message, display name and recent channel messages are sent to it to write a reply.
  • YouTube and similar services: song searches and links, when someone uses music commands.
  • Stripe: payment details, when someone buys Premium.
  • Wispbyte (our hosting provider): stores the data above on its servers.
  • Google: only if you pick "Log in with Google" for the control panel. Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture, and we use them only to log you in.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

🧸Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (older in some countries). The bot is built to be family-friendly, but we don't knowingly collect extra information from children. If you think a child's data is stored, email us and we'll delete it.

🗑️Deleting your data

Server admins can ask us to delete all of their server's data. Members can ask us to delete their XP and warnings. Email moondayapp.help@gmail.com with your Discord user ID or server ID, and we'll do it within 30 days.

Removing the bot from your server stops it collecting anything new there.
