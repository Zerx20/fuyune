Everything your server needs

Moderation Free

Ban, kick, time out and warn in one command. Every action is logged for your staff.

/ban/timeout/warn
🌸

Welcomes Free

Greets every new member with your server's name, gives them a role, and posts rules and role buttons.

/welcome-test
🎫

Tickets Free

One button opens a private chat with your staff. A transcript is saved when it closes.

/ticket-panel

Levels + games Free

XP for chatting, a leaderboard, giveaways, polls, trivia and announcements.

@Fuyune/ask

Songs, playlists and Spotify links in voice channels, with a queue, loop and shuffle.

はじめよう ・ SETUP

After you add her

Move her role up

Server Settings → Roles. Drag Fuyune just below your top staff role so she can moderate.

Make it yours

Open the control panel and set your welcome, goodbye and announcement style. Nothing posts until you turn it on.

Try a command

Type /help, /trivia or /rank. Premium servers can chat with /ask.

Control panel

Server admins log in to change their own server's settings. Visitors can't change anything here.
